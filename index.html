Leonard Rosenthol

PDF Architect · Content Provenance Architect · International Standards Expert

LinkedIn GitHub YouTube PDF Association C2PA

Senior Principal Architect for PDF & the Document Cloud at Adobe, with over 20 years of experience shaping the PDF standard. Project Editor at ISO for PDF/A, PDF/X, and PDF/E, and author of the PDF Electronic Signature standard (PAdES) at ETSI/ESI.

Co-founder and Chair of the Technical Working Group for the Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity (C2PA), which created the industry's first open standard for verifying the origin and history of digital content. Board member of the PDF Association.
